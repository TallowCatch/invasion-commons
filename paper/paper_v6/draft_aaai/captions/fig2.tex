Audits with programmed agents. (a) Share of rounds below the harm line with under-reporters, when nobody is audited, when audits are used without memory, and when the reviewer remembers each caught lie; Fishery and Forest settings differ in the number of greedy agents (2 or 4) and the regrowth rate, and River has three agents per reagent (audits without memory were not run in River). (b) Observed deterrence threshold $e^*$ against the break-even $\gstar$ predicted from runs without audits; filled points are new settings predicted before the search, open points vary the audit rate from $1/6$ to $0.02$ or let audits miss cheats, and the band marks one grid step. (c) Cheaters' gain against the audit rate under five audit rules; the dotted line marks no audits. (d) Harvest per honest agent with a fine of 24 under random audits and audits on a fixed schedule. (e) Harm remaining, relative to no audits, when one audit per round is aimed at a random agent, at the largest report, or at the plot furthest below the reviewer's forecast; numbers give the share of liars ever caught.
