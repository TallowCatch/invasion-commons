Which gain sets the stopping fine. For gpt-oss-120b and Nemotron 3 Super: the gain from over-taking over the whole game and in one round, per over-take round, and the expected fine at which over-taking stopped in the threshold test. Whiskers on the gains show 95\% intervals over populations; the whisker on the stopping fine spans the last fine that did not deter and the first that did. Over-taking stopped near the one-round gain, far above the whole-game gain, which was negative for gpt-oss-120b.
