Observed against predicted stopping fine for the language models. The stopping fine is predicted from each model's observed one-round excess $\hat g_1$ (filled; frozen before the threshold test), from the largest one-round excess available, the $\gone$ of Proposition~\ref{prop:breakeven} (tinted; post hoc), or from the realised whole-game gain of its over-taking (open; post hoc), and observed in the threshold test; points on the dashed line are predicted exactly. Vertical whiskers span the last fine that did not deter and the first that did; horizontal whiskers show 95\% intervals over populations. Grey points: programmed cheaters, predicted from their whole-game gain $\gstar$ (Figure~\ref{fig:audits}b). The observed one-round excess predicted where the language models stopped; the realised whole-game gain did not, and against the largest excess both models stopped early.
