# Slide style guide: Ameer's interview-deck style (the KU deck)

> **For the LLM reading this.** Make or restyle slides so they match this style exactly: the colours, fonts, sizes and layout below. If slides are attached, keep their **content** and change only how they **look**, unless asked otherwise. `examples/contact_sheet.png` shows all eight slides of the reference deck ("Ameer-Alhashemi-KU-PhD-Interview-Deck.pptx", made in Canva).
>
> This is a *different* style from Ameer's TESSERA/lab style (`../style_kit/SLIDE_STYLE_GUIDE.md`). This one has dark slate regular-weight serif headings, Lato text, tan triangle bullets and arrows, and outlined boxes.

Every value below was read from the reference deck's XML and checked against renders of it.

---

## 1. The look in one paragraph

Warm off-white slides. Very **large, regular-weight Georgia headings** in **dark slate** (not bold and not navy). They are usually **centred** at the top, over two lines. **Lato** is used for all running text. Lists use **small tan ▶ triangles** as bullets, or big grey-blue **"01 02 03"** numbers. Ideas are shown as **outlined white boxes** joined by **tan block arrows**, or as **soft grey rounded cards** with centred text. A **heavy, short, near-black bar** appears only on the title slide, under a left-aligned heading, and under "Thank You". The deck closes on a **dark slate slide** with a huge "Thank You". There is plenty of empty space, and no gradients, icons or clip art.

## 2. Page and scale

- **Size:** 16:9. The reference deck is **20 × 11.25 in**, Canva's 1920 × 1080 export. All numbers below are in that native size.
  - **To build at PowerPoint's 13.333 × 7.5 in, multiply every position, size and font size by 2/3.** The table in §4 gives both point sizes.
- **Margins:** content sits about 1.3–2.7 in from the left and right edges (0.85–1.8 in at 13.333 in). It is generous and centred, not packed to the edges.
- **Background:** `#F6F4F1` on every slide except the closing slide.
- **Page number:** bottom right at (18.88, 10.07) in, 20 pt, centred in a tiny box. The colour is black, or `#F6F4F1` on the dark slide.
  - The original font is Canva Sans; use **Lato**.
  - It appears on every slide except the title slide.

## 5. Signature elements

1. **The heavy bar.** A solid rectangle **5.9 × 0.25 in** (3.94 × 0.17 at 13.333 in). It sits about 0.5 in below the text it underlines, aligned with that text's left edge, or centred under centred text.
   - `#111827` on light slides; `#000000` under "Thank You".
   - It is used **only** on the title slide, under a **left-aligned** heading (slide 2) and under "Thank You".
   - Big **centred** headings have **no bar**.
2. **Tan triangle bullets ▶.** A small right-pointing triangle, about 0.33 × 0.41 in, colour `#B29E84`. In the deck it is an image: use `assets/tan_triangle_bullet.png` or `.svg`, or draw an isosceles triangle rotated 90° with the same fill and no outline. It sits left of each bullet paragraph with a gap of about 0.6 in, and bullets are spaced about 1.8 in apart (native size).
3. **Tan block arrows.** Solid right-pointing arrows, about 1.1 × 0.41 in, colour `#B29E84`, with no outline. They link boxes in a flow (A → B → C).
4. **Outlined white boxes.**
   - Fill `#FFFFFF`, a thin **`#7C898B` 1.5 pt** outline, square corners.
   - Used as flow labels (3.6 × 0.93 in, holding one Georgia Bold label) or as process steps (3.4 × 3.6 in).
   - A process step holds a grey "01", a Georgia Bold head and short Lato text, all left-aligned with about 0.4 in of padding.
5. **Soft rounded cards.** Fill `#F3F4F6`, a 1 px `#D1D5DB` border, a large corner radius, about 3.75 × 5.17 in, in a row of four with gaps of about 0.65 in. Each card has a centred Georgia Bold heading and centred grey Lato text.
6. **Tan-bordered callout.** A white box, about 5.3 × 2.55 in, with a **`#B29E84` 3 pt** border. It has a Georgia regular title and a short Lato paragraph, and highlights one key point, such as "Core Contribution".
7. **Big grey numbers "01 02 03".** In Lato, `#7C898B`, right-aligned in a narrow column beside each entry, or top-left inside a process box.

## 6. Slide layouts in the reference deck

Native 20 × 11.25 in coordinates.

1. **Title.**
   - Title in Lato 100 pt, `#253439`, two lines, left-aligned, at (2.32, 4.47), up to 15.3 in wide.
   - The heavy bar starts at the same left edge (x = 2.32, y ≈ 7.85).
   - Footer at the bottom right, right-aligned (box at x = 10.6, y = 9.27, 7.8 in wide): name, then "Institution, date", in Lato 17 pt `#4B5563`.
   - No page number.
2. **Heading left, bullets right, flow along the bottom.**
   - Heading in Georgia 80 pt, two lines, left-aligned at (1.28, 3.6), about 6.6 in wide, with the heavy bar under it from x = 1.28.
   - Right column, x ≈ 10.7, 6.3 in wide: 3–4 bullets with **tan ▶** markers, in Lato 25.7 pt.
   - Bottom row, y ≈ 9.2: three **outlined label boxes** (Georgia Bold 22.7 pt, centred) joined by **tan arrows**.
3. **Rotated side heading, images in the middle, numbered entries on the right.**
   - Heading in Lato 64 pt, rotated −90°, running up the left edge (centre x ≈ 1.7).
   - Middle column, x ≈ 4–10: a stack of 2–3 figure images.
   - Right: 3 entries, each with a grey "01" (Lato 34 pt, right-aligned, x ≈ 10.2–11.7).
   - Each entry, at x = 12.18 and 6.7 in wide, has a Georgia Bold 24 pt title, then 2 "•" bullets in Lato 20.75 pt, justified. The second bullet is in **italic**, naming the project and where it was accepted.
4. **Centred heading, tan bullets on the left, figures and a callout on the right.**
   - Centred heading in Georgia 80 pt, two lines (box at y = 0.73, 2.27 in tall).
   - Left, x = 1.82: 4 bullets with tan ▶ markers, in Lato 21.7 pt.
   - Top right: a figure with a centred caption.
   - Bottom left: a wide strip of figure panels with a caption.
   - Bottom right: a **tan-bordered callout**.
5. **Centred heading, a question, four rounded cards.**
   - Centred heading.
   - A single centred question line (Lato 18 pt) at y ≈ 3.5.
   - A row of **4 rounded cards** at y ≈ 4.95. Each has a centred Georgia Bold 28 pt title and centred grey Lato 23.6 pt text.
6. **Centred heading and a five-step process.**
   - Centred heading.
   - **Outlined process boxes** (3.4 × 3.6 in): 3 on the top row (y ≈ 3.25) and 2 on the bottom row (y ≈ 7.26), offset to sit between the top ones.
   - **Tan arrows** run between neighbouring boxes.
   - In each box: a grey number (Lato 22.7 pt), a Georgia Bold 22.7 pt head and Lato 15.6 pt text.
7. **Centred heading and two bullet columns.**
   - Centred heading.
   - Two columns at x = 2.23 and x = 11.15, from y ≈ 4.1. Each has a Georgia Bold 32 pt sub-head and 3 "•" bullets in Lato 22.3 pt (navy `#062746`).
   - One centred closing sentence at y ≈ 9.05, in Lato 18 pt.
8. **Closing.**
   - Background `#253439`.
   - "Thank You" in Lato 144 pt, `#F6F4F1`, centred over two lines (box at x = 5.38, y = 3.19).
   - A heavy **black** bar centred under it (x ≈ 7.05–12.96, y ≈ 7.85).
   - The date rotated 90° near the right edge (Lato 24 pt).
   - Name and email at the bottom left (1.12, 9.55), Lato 17 pt, white.
   - Page number.

When making new slides, reuse these eight patterns. For a single large figure, use a centred heading with the figure centred below it and a caption, plus an optional tan-bordered callout.

## 7. How the words go on the page

- **Headings** are 2–6 words, large, and usually centred over two lines. Sentence case ("Proposed PhD direction") and Title Case ("Academic Background") both appear; pick one per deck and keep it.
- **Bullets** are 1–2 lines each, at most 3–4 per column. Each starts with a verb or a fact ("Developed …", "First-Class MEng …").
- **Names of projects, papers and venues** go in *italic* inside bullets.
- **Box and card text** is short: a 2–3 word head, then 1–3 short lines.
- **One idea per slide,** with lots of air. Shorten text rather than shrink it: never go below about 15 pt native (10 pt at 13.333 in) for body text.
- **Figures** sit plainly, with no frames except a thin outline when the figure has a white background. Each gets a one-line centred caption.

## 8. Don'ts

- No bold headings and no navy headings: headings are **regular Georgia in `#253439`**.
- No accent colour other than tan `#B29E84`, and no gradients, shadows, icons or emoji.
- No bar under centred headings.
- No Calibri headings and no Arial body text; use Lato.
- Don't fill the slide edge to edge: keep the wide, centred margins.
