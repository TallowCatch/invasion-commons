## Reproducibility Caveats

- `results/` is ignored by git, but the current paper's fresh confirmation
  cohort is now included as a compressed source bundle under the paper data
  directory. The historical exploratory runs listed above are not all
  included, so reproducing every earlier result still needs separate inputs.
- Figure 3 currently uses Stage A aggregate summaries; run-level uncertainty should be added where available.
- The LLM bridge figure is intentionally limited to patch-health panels. The collapse summary is carried by the caption, the LLM bridge outcome table, and surrounding text rather than by a footer inside the figure.
- Broad threshold sensitivity for the local safety margin and global patch-health threshold is now available from the recovered full-grid sweep. The paper includes the extracted example-trace check and the full threshold robustness table/figure. Additional sensitivity would require varying the failed-patch threshold or using alternative aggregate safety predicates.
