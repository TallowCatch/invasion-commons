## Verbatim quotes (working-paper abstract)

1. > "We collect individual participant data from 70 papers that use laboratory experiments to examine individual tax evasion behavior (or "Tax Evasion Games"), in order to use meta-analysis to estimate the impacts of diﬀerent public policy, experimental design and individual level variables on tax evasion choices."
2. > "Our results show that standard enforcement variables like audits (including audit rules) and ﬁnes perform diﬀerently on the extensive and intensive margins."
3. > "Our results also indicate that the demographic characteristics of the subjects (e.g., gender, experimental income, occupation, risk attitude) aﬀect compliance."

## Relevance
The definitive survey of lab audit experiments (the "Tax Evasion Game" built on Allingham & Sandmo 1972). It is the right citation for "the audit probabilities and fines we use are within the range used in 70 lab studies", but the range itself must be read from the paper's tables before Ameer cites it. The extensive-versus-intensive-margin result (audits change whether people cheat and how much differently) suggests reporting both "share of agents over-taking" and "amount over-taken".
