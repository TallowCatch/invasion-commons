## Verbatim quotes

1. Where the lab CPR game's structure comes from:
   > "The structure of Gordon's time-independent model (1954) has been used as the foundation to create a series of baseline laboratory experiments that examine the empirical generality of the conventional theory [Walker, Gardner and Ostrom (1990)]."
2. Parameterisation:
   > "In these experiments, eight subjects are similarly endowed with either 10 or 25 tokens in each period of a finitely repeated game. Any or all of these tokens can be invested in a joint activity with the mathematical structure of a quadratic production function (the common-pool resource) or in an alternative activity that generates a fixed return per token (similar to investing time in wage labor)."
3. The dilemma is certified by the gap between equilibrium and optimum:
   > "Given the payoff parameters, a group investment of 36 tokens yields the optimal level of investment. The noncooperative Nash equilibrium for a finitely repeated game is for each subject to invest 8 tokens in the common-pool resource (regardless of the number of tokens provided as an endowment)."
   > "The outcome reached at the predicted Nash equilibrium is 39 percent of the joint optimum that could be earned."
4. Sanctioning treatment (fee-to-fine), after 10 baseline rounds:
   > "These assign them the capability to incur a cost (a fee) after each appropriation decision in order to sanction another subject (who then is charged a fine)."
5. Results by institution:
   > "The average net yield increases from 21 percent with no sanctioning to 37 percent with sanctioning. When the costs of fees and fines are subtracted from average net yield, however, net yield drops to 9 percent."
   > "With an imposed sanctioning mechanism and only a single opportunity to communicate, subjects achieve an average net yield of 85 percent."
   With communication plus a voted sanctioning system: 93 percent gross, 90 percent net, defection rate 4 percent.

## Relevance
The canonical CPR lab game is grounded in Gordon's (1954) open-access fishery model and its parameters were chosen so that Nash (64 tokens) and optimum (36 tokens) are far apart (Nash earns 39% of optimum); the outcome is reported as "net yield as a percentage of optimum", net of sanction costs. This is the human-lab precedent for Ameer's welfare metric: report efficiency relative to the optimum, and subtract enforcement costs (fees, fines, audits) to get net yield.
