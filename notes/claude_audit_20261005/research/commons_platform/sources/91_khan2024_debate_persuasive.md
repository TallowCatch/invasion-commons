## Relevance to the commons overseer

Information asymmetry is the key design feature: experts see the text, judges do not. A commons overseer is in the same position (agents know their true catch; the overseer sees reports and samples). The 'optimise debaters for persuasiveness' step is the analogue of a red team that optimises agents to talk their way out of fines.
