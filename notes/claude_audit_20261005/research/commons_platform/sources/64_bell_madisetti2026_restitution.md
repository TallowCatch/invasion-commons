## Verbatim quotes (from the abstract on the ICML page)

1. > "However, we observed that in a standard common-pool fishing scenario, a single adversarial agent"
   > "(for example a greedy outsider) causes cooperation to collapse and the fish stock to fall to 0 within 2-3 rounds."
2. > "Informal governance (for example social pressure) fails to prevent this collapse."
3. > "In contrast, a formal enforcement mechanism, where a majority vote immediately returns the sanctioned agent's catch"
   > "to the pool, makes cooperation robust to adversarial outsiders, yielding a 2.7-4.3 times improvement"
   > "across four families of models (n=8 seeds, p<0.015)."
4. > "Restitution (returning the sanctioned agent's catch) works well, while ejecting the outsider"
   > "or delaying restitution reduces effectiveness."
5. > "This contrast indicates that formal enforcement works because it does not rely on social responsiveness."

## Relevance
"A standard common-pool fishing scenario" with a "greedy outsider" strongly suggests GovSim's fishery and its outsider perturbation (61), but this is inferred from the abstract wording, not confirmed. The closest published LLM work to Ameer's overseer: a formal sanction (restitution by majority vote) against an adversarial agent, compared with ejection and delay, with 8 seeds. The exact stock, regrowth and sanction parameters could not be checked.
