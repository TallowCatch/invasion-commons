## Relevance to the commons overseer

Gives a collusion metric that is computable in a commons: the coalition's gain (or the group's loss) relative to the cooperative optimum, measured on actions, with an LLM judge on messages kept separate. 'Collusion on paper' warns that talk about cheating and actual over-taking must be measured separately.
