## Relevance to the commons overseer

Classical source for audits as a game with a limited inspection budget and inspector leadership (commitment). AI control's 'blue team commits, red team best-responds' [80, 81] is the same leader–follower structure, so the commons overseer can be framed as an inspector-leadership game. Only the abstract was read; specific theorems should be checked in the chapter before citing them.
