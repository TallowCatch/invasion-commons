## Relevance

An LLM Common Harvest variant with two regions and region-level navigation (an abstraction of the grid, not a Melting Pot or SocialJax map, as far as the extracted text shows), two-player and heterogeneous-group settings, 200 steps. It supports the pattern that LLM commons work either abstracts space or pays for per-step play. No overseer.
