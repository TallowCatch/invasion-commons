# AI safety via debate

- **Authors:** Geoffrey Irving, Paul Christiano, Dario Amodei
- **Year:** 2018
- **Venue / identifier:** arXiv:1805.00899
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.00899
- **Source type:** Preprint (proposal + toy experiment)
- **Date accessed:** 14 Oct 2026
- **Depth read:** Abstract only.

## Verbatim quotes

1. "we propose training agents via self play on a zero sum debate game"
2. "two agents take turns making short statements up to a limit, then a human judges which of the agents gave the most true, useful information"
3. "debate with optimal play can answer any question in PSPACE given polynomial time judges (direct judging answers only NP questions)"

## Relevance to the commons overseer

Origin of debate as an oversight protocol: a weak judge can rule correctly if two strong adversaries argue. In the commons, a debate variant is an accused agent and an accuser (another agent or a prosecutor model) arguing before a weak overseer whether an over-take happened, with the judge's accuracy against ground truth as the metric.
