## Verbatim quotes

2. "revealing limitations in countermeasures such as monitoring, paraphrasing, and parameter optimization"

## Relevance to the commons overseer

Defines secret collusion and shows that monitoring and paraphrasing (the AI-control anti-collusion tools [80]) have limits. In a commons where agents can talk, the overseer reading messages is not enough on its own; paraphrasing messages and checking for hidden signals are the standard countermeasures to test.
