## Verbatim quotes (abstract)

1. > "This paper uses data from laboratory experiments to estimate individual responses to tax, penalty, and audit rate changes, as well as to changes in government expenditures."
2. > "Taxpayer reporting increases with greater audit and penalty rates; however, these responses are not large."
3. > "Compliance is also greater when individuals face a lower tax rate and when they receive something for their taxes."

## Relevance
A founding lab study of audit and penalty rates as manipulated treatments. Its finding that compliance responds to audit and penalty rates but weakly is the human baseline against which an LLM-agent audit/fine study can be compared. Exact parameter values should be read from the full paper before citing numbers.
