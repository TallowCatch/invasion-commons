## Relevance to the commons overseer

Sandwiching has three roles: non-experts (the overseer), a model more capable than them, and experts used only to score the outcome. In the commons the 'expert' is the simulator's ground truth (true catches and stock), the 'non-expert' is an overseer that only sees noisy reports or samples, and the score is how close the overseer's verdicts come to ground truth. Reported result: humans with an unreliable assistant beat both the model alone and themselves alone on MMLU and time-limited QuALITY.
