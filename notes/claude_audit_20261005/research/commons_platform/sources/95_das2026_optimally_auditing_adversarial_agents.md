## Relevance to the commons overseer

Formal model of audit design with many strategic agents: the principal commits to audit probabilities per reported type, agents play the worst equilibrium for the principal, with either a per-audit cost or a hard budget. It gives algorithms for optimal audit vectors and a no-regret online version. This is the right theory reference for type-dependent audit targeting (Ameer's T1) and for treating colluding agents as choosing the worst equilibrium.
