## Relevance to the commons overseer

Shows how scalable-oversight protocols are compared: fix a weak judge, vary protocol (debate, consultancy, direct QA; assigned vs open roles) and agent strength, and report judge accuracy. The commons version: fix a small overseer model, vary the oversight protocol (rule, LLM reading reports, LLM with debate between accused and accuser) and the agents' model size, and report the overseer's verdict accuracy and the harm that follows.
