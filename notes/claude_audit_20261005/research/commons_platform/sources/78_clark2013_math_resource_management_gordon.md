## Relevance
Confirms that the fisheries-economics standard is logistic growth rx(1 − x/K) with MSY = rK/4 at x = K/2 (the parabola's maximum), and that Gordon's open-access equilibrium (where rent is driven to zero) is the reason commons over-harvest. Both the human CPR lab game (71, 72: "based on Gordon's (1954) classic model") and GovSim (60 cites Gordon) trace back here. Ameer's logistic resource with an MSY harm line is therefore on the fisheries-science standard; what needs citing is the choice of r and K (see 79) and the time step.
