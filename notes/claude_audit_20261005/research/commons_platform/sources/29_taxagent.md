# 29. TaxAgent: How Large Language Model Designs Fiscal Policy

- **Authors:** Jizhou Wang, Xiaodan Fang, Lei Huang, Yongfeng Huang
- **Year / venue:** 2025, arXiv:2506.02838 (3 Jun 2025); arXiv comment "Accepted as oral presentation at ICME 2025"
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/html/2506.02838v1
- **Source type:** academic
- **Accessed:** 14 Oct 2026

## Verbatim quotes

1. "In our macroeconomic simulation, heterogeneous H-Agents (households) simulate real-world taxpayer behaviors while the TaxAgent (government) utilizes LLMs to iteratively optimize tax rates, balancing equity and productivity."
2. "Benchmarked against Saez Optimal Taxation, U.S. federal income taxes, and free markets, TaxAgent achieves superior equity-efficiency trade-offs."
3. "One H-Agent represents one household, using an LLM to model decision-making in response to economic conditions."
4. Related work: "For example, “The AI Economist” framework uses RL to co-adapt agents and social planners [ 16 ] ."

## Relevance

LLM government plus LLM households, evaluated on the AI Economist's baseline set (free market, U.S. federal, Saez) and the equality–productivity trade-off. It is a macroeconomic (non-spatial) simulation, not Gather-Trade-Build. It confirms that "LLM planner" work since 2024 has left the 2D grid behind.
