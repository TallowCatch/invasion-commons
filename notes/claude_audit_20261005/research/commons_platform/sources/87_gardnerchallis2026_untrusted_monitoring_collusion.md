## Relevance to the commons overseer

Gives the taxonomy of how an untrusted monitor can collude with the policy it monitors. For a commons with an LLM overseer from the same family as the agents, this is the list of collusion routes to test: does a same-family overseer go easier on same-family agents (passive self-recognition), can agents pre-agree a signal, and can they coordinate on a Schelling point without talking.
