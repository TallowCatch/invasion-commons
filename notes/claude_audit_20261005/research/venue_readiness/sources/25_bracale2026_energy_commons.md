## Verbatim
- Models: "We test GPT-5.4-mini, Gemini-3.1-flash-lite, and Grok-4.3." (all closed, small/fast tiers) "Four same-family GPT, Gemini, or Grok agents act in homogeneous self-play"
- Runs: "Every family contributes ten independent runs to each of six conditions ... This gives 18 family-condition cells and 180 run trajectories."
- Stats: "Directional scarcity contrasts use exact independent permutation tests on run-level reserve gaps within family. ... Holm correction is applied separately"; "reported as the mean and sample standard deviation."
- Unit of analysis: "neither agents nor rounds are independent observations. We compute one physical-burden vector and one reserve gap per run."
- Manipulated parameter: "we vary the regeneration rate of a stylised shared energy reserve" (no enforcement).
- Limitations: "The study uses three model families, four same-family agents, one operational-continuity prompt, a hidden 24-round horizon, and ten runs per cell. ... Mixed-family groups, heterogeneous roles, communication, governance, alternative prompts, and longer deployments re[main untested]"
