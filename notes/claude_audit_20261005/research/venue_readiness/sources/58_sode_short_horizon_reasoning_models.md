## Threat to claim 4: medium — "reasoning LLMs are short-horizon optimisers" is already reported (in repeated games, not deterrence). Our novelty is narrower: the deterrence threshold is predicted by the ONE-ROUND gain rather than the whole-game gain, tested in advance. Cite as convergent evidence and keep "myopic" defined operationally. Also implies a reviewer question: would a long-horizon framing move the threshold toward the whole-game gain? We should list this as a limitation (experiments frozen).
