## JAIR (official)
- Scope: all areas of AI "including agents and multi-agent systems". No page limit; welcomes short papers and research notes.
- Turnaround: reviews "within approximately three months of submission" (as returned).
- Criteria: originality, significance, clarity; claims "supported by empirical experiments or theoretical analysis, with reproducible experiments"; papers "should report what was learned, not just what was done."
## AIJ (official, 2021 page)
- "AIJ considers eligible for publication mature high quality work that is complete and novel." Typical length "between twenty and thirty standard (A4 or Letter size) pages". Research Notes up to 4500 words, reviewed faster. Turnaround not stated officially; LetPub (third-party) says ~9 months average. Conference papers "rarely" complete without added material. Fit: MED (long, slow).
## JAAMAS (secondary)
- Official journal of IFAAMAS; foundations, theory and practice of agents and multi-agent systems; Springer page reportedly shows median 12 days to first decision (desk decision?), LetPub says >12 weeks average peer review. Editors per search: Michael Winikoff, Kate Larson. Fit: HIGH on scope; no length/criteria verified.
## JASSS (secondary)
- Quarterly open-access journal on social simulation; double-anonymous; at least two referees; ~10 weeks refereeing (older listing); APC EUR 1000 / GBP 800 / USD 1300 (aggregator; verify). Requires replication info. Fit: MED (simulation of CPR enforcement fits; LLM-agent angle less so).
## PNAS Nexus (official, scope only)
- Quote: "Of particular interest are articles with broad, interdisciplinary appeal at the intersection of one or more disciplines." Subject areas include Economic Sciences, Computer Sciences. Length/time (third-party: ~6000-word main text, ~1.5 months to first decision) unverified. Fit: LOW-MED.
