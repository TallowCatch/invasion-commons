# AAAI-27 review process: double-blind and arXiv policy
- URL: https://aaai.org/conference/aaai/aaai-27/review-process/
- Accessed: 2026-10-12
- Source type: primary/official
- Scores: credibility 5 / recency 5 / bias 1
- Caveat: extracted via WebFetch summary; spot-check wording.

## Verbatim quotes (as returned)
- "AAAI-27 reviews are double blind (i.e., neither reviewers nor authors should be able to identify each other)."
- Not treated as a blind-review violation: "posting a preliminary version as an unrefereed preprint, such as on arXiv, social media, or a personal website, or in a non-archival workshop" (paraphrase by fetch tool; exact wording not confirmed) and discussing the work in research talks.
- Conditions: the submission must not cite or point to that non-anonymous material; the online material must not state that the work was submitted to AAAI-27; violations may lead to summary rejection.
- Papers revealing author/affiliation may be rejected without review.

## Bottom line
arXiv posting is permitted at AAAI, ICML (source 06), NeurIPS (source 08), ICLR (source 09), AAMAS (source 10), TMLR (source 17), AIES (source 13), provided the arXiv version does not say it is under review at the venue and the submission does not cite it.
