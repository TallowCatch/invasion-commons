# AAAI-27 workshop list: workshops relevant to this paper
- URLs: https://aaai.org/conference/aaai/aaai-27/aaai-27-workshop-program-list/ (list; read in two chunks, 42 workshops) plus individual workshop sites below
- Accessed: 2026-10-12
- Source type: primary/official (AAAI list) and organiser sites (primary for each workshop)
- Scores: credibility 4-5 / recency 5 / bias 1-2
- Caveat: WebFetch summaries. Dates for some workshops differ between AAAI list and workshop site (flagged).

## Best fits (all deadlines Nov 20, 2026 AoE unless noted; notification Dec 2, 2026; workshop Feb 22-23, 2027, Montreal)
1. WMAC 2027: Measuring Collective Intelligence in Agentic Systems. https://multiagents.org/2027/ . "Double-blind review on OpenReview, with three reviews per paper." Non-archival. Full up to 8 pages, short/position/demo up to 4 pages. Deadline Nov 20, 2026; notification Dec 2. Organisers expect 40-50 submissions, accept 15-20. Fit: HIGH (multi-agent coordination/collective behaviour).
2. Social Choice for AI Ethics and Safety 2027 (SC4AI'27). https://sites.google.com/view/sc4ai/workshops/sc4ai27 . Non-archival ("Previously published or concurrently submitted work is welcome"). Full 6-8, short 2-4 pages excl. refs. OpenReview. Scope: pluralistic alignment, collective oversight, mechanism design for human-AI and AI-AI interaction. Fit: MED-HIGH (mechanism design, oversight).
3. 3rd Social Simulation with LLMs Workshop. https://sites.google.com/view/social-sims-with-llms/social-sim27 . Scope quote: organised around "population construction, collective dynamics, and consequences for evaluation, safety, and governance". Workshop site says submission Nov 20, 2026 (AoE), camera-ready Dec 9; the AAAI list page says "Submission deadline Nov 6, 2026 (GMT-5)", submissions open Oct 9. DISCREPANCY: assume Nov 6 to be safe. Pages (AAAI list): regular 7, early-stage/position 4. Fit: MED-HIGH (LLM agent societies, governance).
4. Who Does Your AI Agent Work For? (Loyal Agents). https://loyalagents.github.io/aaai27 . Call "not yet open" at access; expected papers due Nov 20, 2026; up to 8 pages per AAAI list. Scope: delegation, permission controls, economics, law. Fit: MED.
5. Trustworthy Evolution of AI Agents. https://trustworthy-agent-evolution.github.io . Full 8 / short 4 pages excl. refs, Nov 20. Fit: LOW-MED (agent safety, monitoring; less about CPR enforcement).
6. Security and Privacy Foundations for LLMs (SPF-LLM); Human-AI Collaboration: Partnership, Complementarity and Shared Agency; Multi-Objective and Multi-Task Optimization for Robust AI; Disinformation under Generative AI Safety; Embodied AI Meta-alignment: LOW fit.
- Not found in the AAAI-27 list: a dedicated cooperative-AI, AI-governance, or economics-and-computation workshop.
