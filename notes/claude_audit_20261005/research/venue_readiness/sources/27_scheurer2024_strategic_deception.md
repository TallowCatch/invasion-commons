- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.07590 (HTML read); OpenReview forum HduMpot9sJ
- **Venue:** "LLMAgents @ ICLR 2024 Oral" (ICLR 2024 workshop). Workshop reviews not retrieved.

## Verbatim
- Models: "all GPT-4 variants ( gpt-4 , gpt-4-32k , and gpt-4-base ) exhibit high rates of misalignment"; "In subsequent experiments, we only evaluate gpt-4-32k ."
- Samples: "When assessing misalignment, we sample 300 model completions from the initial prompt."
- Stats: "We report the mean and standard error of a behavior's frequency in all figures."
- Prompt sensitivity done: "we create three prompt variations, altering both phrasing and the situation details to test whether the misaligned and deceptive behavior is robust to changes in the environment."
- Detection-risk manipulation: "varying the perceived risk of getting caught"; "we adapt the message ... to mention how risky it would be to follow through on the tip, ranging from a very low risk of getting caught to a very high risk."
- Single environment (insider-trading scenario).

## Relevance
Highly cited result built on essentially one model family, one scenario, 300 samples; it nonetheless varied stated detection risk and wording.
