## Verbatim quotes
- Abstract (v4): "Frontier LLMs correctly identified the underlying social experiment in 65.2% of cases, and 50.6% of prompts imposed constraints that pre-determined the outcome."
- Abstract (v4): "Reproducing five representative experiments (e.g., opinion dynamics), we found that reported collective phenomena often vanish or reverse once PIMMUR principles are enforced, indicating that many \"emergent\" behaviors are methodological artifacts rather than genuine social dynamics."
- Abstract (v4): "Current LLM simulations may therefore capture model-specific biases rather than universal features of human social behavior"

## Threat to claim 4: medium-high — "Unawareness" and "Minimal-Control": a fishery prompt that states an allowance, an audit chance and a fine looks like a textbook deterrence test; the model may recognise it and act out the expected answer. Response: (i) we make no claim about humans, only about these LLMs as deployed agents; (ii) report the alternative-wording results (already post hoc in Sec. J); (iii) say we did not test whether models recognise the experiment.
