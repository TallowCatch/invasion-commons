# Current Research Review

Updated 23 September 2026. Start with the completed evidence, then read the
older plans for context. The working manuscript includes the fresh-seed
matched-information result as a bounded mechanism study; the older pilot
results remain in their closeouts.

## Current question

When agents' resource-use decisions interact, how much request information
and checking effort does a monitor need to catch harmful proposals without
unnecessarily blocking useful ones? The comparison holds the safety target
and intervention menu fixed. Fishery tests shared-stock accounting; Harvest
tests spatial spillovers and uncertain renewal. This is not yet a validated
scalar actor-overseer capability gap or a live-LLM oversight result.

## Latest completed stages

1. [Matched oversight pilot](MATCHED_OVERSIGHT_IMPLEMENTATION_CLOSEOUT.md):
   common target and authority, paired policy contexts, direct decision logs.
2. [Decision-case coverage](DECISION_CASE_COVERAGE_CLOSEOUT.md):
   constructed safe/risky cases, native replay and strong local baselines.
3. [Held-out policies](HELDOUT_POLICY_PILOT_CLOSEOUT.md): newly seeded complete
   episodes; mixed-policy repair gives two-class coverage in informative cells.
4. [Budgeted reviewers](BUDGETED_REVIEWER_PILOT_CLOSEOUT.md): 400 episodes,
   request-inspection budgets 0/3/6, 1,716 frozen proposals and 15,444 matched
   decisions. More shared request information reduced safe rejections in
   Fishery, while a strong local Harvest rule remained competitive. Four
   policy contexts per mixture make this a development result.
5. [Cleanup admission](CLEANUP_PARSER_REPAIR_CLOSEOUT.md): native adapter and
   observation-only policies were tested, but the productive policy never
   reached the declared safe capacity or harvested; Cleanup is not admitted
   for cross-game oversight comparison.
6. [Fresh-seed reviewer confirmation](BUDGETED_REVIEWER_CONFIRMATION_CLOSEOUT.md):
   the primary question is immediate decision quality at a fixed safety
   target. Two pilot-selected settings were tested with 64 new population
   contexts each, all three information budgets and both local comparators.
   Joint review improves on conservative local review's safe-request
   retention, but does not dominate optimistic local review in Harvest.
7. [Actor-search pressure pilot](ACTOR_PRESSURE_PILOT_CLOSEOUT.md): nested
   1/8/32-candidate strategy search against fixed Harvest reviewers changed
   held-out actor payoff unevenly. The four-context pilot did not show a
   monotone safety effect or harder reviewer errors; do not use it as a
   validated capability-gap result. Its prospective local contract is
   [here](ACTOR_PRESSURE_PILOT_PROTOCOL.md).

The governing design is

## Remaining paper gate

The immediate decision trade-off has now been predeclared and tested on fresh
contexts, while long-run resource/welfare remains secondary. The working
manuscript treats it as a mechanism study; supervisor feedback should decide
whether the final paper retains the broader benchmark frame or becomes a
narrower oversight-information paper. The confirmation holds actors fixed.
The later actor-search pilot varies one search resource, but does not produce
a validated monotone safety or reviewer-error pressure axis. A separate
prospective design must produce informative held-out actor proposals before a
larger actor-by-reviewer study. Keep inspection, model checks and
communication separate rather than collapsing them into one score. Cleanup
still needs a new policy/starting-state design before cross-game comparison.
